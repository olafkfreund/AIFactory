# Intent: Reaper must not fail a build whose dispatch is still in flight

## Problem

The kubejob reconciler (`reap_vanished_jobs`) failed a build in the same second it was dispatched. Admission writes a `running` row with a pending worker ref; dispatch then populates and packs the worktree and only afterwards creates the Job. In that window the reaper finds no Job under the reconstructed name and applies the #1606 rule "no worker ever claimed this slot". The task went to human_review/errors for the whole 16 min build.

## Proposed outcome

A row that was admitted recently is never reaped for a missing Job. A genuinely crashed dispatch (no Job, row older than a grace) is still reaped, so the #1606 capacity fix keeps working.

## Affected users and systems

`apps/web-server/server/services/build_backend.py` (reconciler), task status shown to users and CFactory.

## Constraints

Must keep #857 (Job status wins) and #1606 (crashed dispatch frees its slot). No new storage or schema.

## Open questions

None. Decision (owner delegation 2026-11-18): grace measured from the admission row's `updated_at`, not a separate marker, because that row already is the in-flight marker written before packing; least risky.
